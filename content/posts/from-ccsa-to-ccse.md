## A new milestone

I completed my Check Point Certified Security Expert (CCSE) certification in October 2026, following CCSA R81 in 2025. It is a welcome milestone in my work across network security and infrastructure engineering.

My professional work brings together two closely connected areas: keeping applications and infrastructure running, and protecting the systems they depend on. The certification adds to that foundation.

## Connecting study with real environments

Through my security engineering engagement with Etech International, I have worked on Check Point infrastructure modernization, cluster validation and controlled maintenance. That includes gateway refresh work, policy verification and failover checks.

These areas make it clear why security administration extends beyond a successful configuration change. High availability, recovery planning and useful documentation all matter to the people who operate the environment afterward.

## Continuing the work

CCSE is a step in an ongoing learning journey. Alongside my professional work, I am pursuing an MSc in Information Technology and Cybersecurity at Islington College, affiliated with London Metropolitan University.

This journal will be a place to share selected lessons, project notes and further learning across DevOps, systems and security. I will keep client-specific configurations and internal operational details out of public posts.
